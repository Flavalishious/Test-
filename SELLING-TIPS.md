1. **Direct Sales to Clients**: $150-500

2. **Marketplace (Gumroad, Framer Marketplace, etc.)**: $29-79

## Handling Objections

### "Isn't this just ChatGPT?"
"This is specifically built for Framer, with lead capture and production deployment. ChatGPT doesn't integrate with your site or collect emails."

### "How much will OpenAI cost me?"
"About $6-10/month for typical usage (100 visitors/day). That's 10x cheaper than Intercom or Drift."

### "Can't I build this myself?"
"Of course! This just saves you 10-20 hours of development time. Plus, you get the Cloudflare Worker setup and documentation."

### "What if I need help?"
"I include email support and comprehensive documentation. Plus, the Framer and OpenAI communities are super helpful."
